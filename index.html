About MSA Enterprise LLC

MSA Enterprise LLC (AKHIYA ENTERPRISES LLC) is an Oman-based transportation and logistics company specializing in heavy transport, freight movement, import & export, and GCC cross-border logistics. Operating from Muscat and Sohar, we help businesses move cargo safely and efficiently across Oman and GCC markets.
